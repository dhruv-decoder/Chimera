Instant, irrevocable settlement makes real-time rails the highest-velocity
laundering surface. India scale in 2026: 524,121 suspected mule accounts flagged
in March 2026 alone; 2.47M Layer-1 mule accounts flagged nationally; NPCI's
MuleHunter.AI has detected 470k+ mule accounts, yet new accounts onboard daily.
Indians lost ~Rs 22,495 crore (~$2.7B) to cyber fraud in 2025, complaints up 24%
to ~2.81M.

Dominant patterns:
- Authorised push payment (APP) scams: deepfake voice/video (e.g. "digital
  arrest", family-emergency, fake-executive pretexts) induce the victim to
  authorise the transfer themselves - auth controls cannot stop a genuine
  authorisation.
- Mule networks: fan-in collection then fan-out dispersion, funds layered within
  minutes; young accounts with immediate high throughput; tight community
  structure.
- Structuring: transfers sized just under reporting/velocity thresholds, fanned
  across payees and time.

RBI's Digital Payments Intelligence Platform shares verified fraud signals across
banks in real time; RBI is weighing added friction (delays on large first-time
transfers, a transaction "kill switch") - trading UPI's speed for safety. This
maps directly to a risk-based mitigation policy: allow / step-up / hold / block.
